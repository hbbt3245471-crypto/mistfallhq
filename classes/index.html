Mistfall Hunter Classes: Six Official Gyldhunters

Mistfall Hunter has six official classes, each with two stances. Compare Mercenary through Withered Knight from Steam patches, not community letter ranks.

Mistfall Hunter classes are a closed official set of six. The Steam store promises six distinct classes, two unique weapon stances each, and a mid-fight swap. Steam news since launch then names the six: Mercenary, Blackarrow, Shadowstrix, Sorcerer, Seer, and Withered Knight. Xbox Wire repeats the “six distinctive classes” line. There is no seventh live class.

This mistfall hunter classes page stays inside those official names. It will not invent a hidden kit, a letter rank, or a redeem-code reroll. There are no official class codes. Bellring has not published a tier list.

What official copy actually promises

The store is the design contract. You pick a class, then customize it with talent trees, gem affixes, and active skills. The advertised identities are assault, maneuvering, crowd control, and defense. Official copy also says you can break free from rigid class restraints by swapping stances in a fight.

The official website is shorter. Select a class, use magic and steel, and switch weapons during battles. That is the whole public class tutorial. Anything more specific comes from patch notes, not from a class encyclopedia Bellring has posted.

Balance is unfinished on purpose. The August 6 notes warn that a class missing from a patch is still under review, not “perfect.” The August 12 notes say the team is monitoring the meta, including areas not covered that day. Read every community ranking as a snapshot of one writer’s week.

The six official classes

Mercenary is the official frontline starter in press coverage. Steam confirms Mercenary Sword and Shield, including an August 6 fix for unwanted auto-blocks. Game Rant describes a second hammer identity and calls Mercenary the best overall starting class. Official store text only promises two stances, so treat the hammer as 待确认 until you confirm it in the skill screen. Official notes do not give Mercenary the highest HP in the game.

Blackarrow is the official bow class. August 6 removed impact from uncharged Steel Arrow shots and reduced impact on full charges. August 12 then lengthened several arrow cooldowns, shortened Sonic Arrow, and reduced Blackarrow’s PvP damage multiplier in solo mode while leaving PvE damage unchanged. Official balance now treats solo PvP as a different number from PvE.

Sorcerer is the official spell class. Launch notes mention a Stardust playstyle, a charged Meteor Charm, and a Chant Guard talent for Stardust spells. August 12 retuned Fire Bolt, Flameblade, Crystal Icebolt, Deep Freeze, and Thunderstrike, and it also cut Sorcerer’s solo PvP damage multiplier. If you want a first character, that PvP cut is a warning, not a dare.

Shadowstrix is the official ambush class. August 12 touched Spinning Slash, Sleight of Hand, Phantom Shift, Battle Frenzy, Inspiring Impale, Wound Accumulation, and Aftereffect. Game Rant calls it the mobility and single-target specialist with stealth. Official notes do not publish a stealth duration or a burst multiplier.

Seer is the official support-leaning class. August 12 fixed a Reverent Seer Punishment Rune that could lock onto Richie the merchant, which at least confirms a Reverent path and rune tools. Game Rant describes life-and-death magic, totems, runes, and sigils. Official public notes do not rank Seer for solo play.

Withered Knight is the other official frontline class. The July 17 launch announcement and the July 30 Games Press release add a Polearm and Shield archetype built for balanced attack and defense. Launch notes buffed that line: cheaper Charged Dash energy, better low-tier turning, stronger basic thrusts, a stronger Spear Barrage multiplier, cheaper Rainbow Piercer, and cheaper Block. Game Rant still talks about a two-hander and a Wither debuff, so confirm both stances in the skill screen.

How to choose without a fake seventh class

Open the class screen and look for two stances you are willing to practice. Official design wants you to swap mid-fight, so a class whose second stance bores you is a worse pick than a “lower tier” class you will actually swap on. Then play the Training Room if the menus offer it. Launch notes mention public Training Room settings, which means the mode exists.

If you want the press consensus first hunt, pick Mercenary. That is Game Rant’s launch-day opinion, not a Bellring letter. If you already know the extract loop, the same press piece still likes Withered Knight, Blackarrow, and Shadowstrix for different jobs. Those opinions move when patches land.

Best class for Soul Harvest is 待确认. The ritual can trigger in Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde, cocoon holders reveal each other, and the Soulgnawer is the named seasonal threat. Official notes do not assign a class to that fight. Best class for Brandrgarde-Cataclysm is also 待确认. Best class for Brandrgarde Solo Mode is not a live question, because that queue is scheduled for mid Season 1 in September.

Do not invent a seventh class from a roadmap teaser. The July 17 launch announcement said a new Blackarrow weapon archetype would launch in a future season, not as a new class. Game Rant’s later roadmap recap names a Javelin for Season 2. That weapon name is 待确认 until a Steam note prints it. It is still one of the six classes.

What official notes will not tell you

Bellring’s own patches speak in “slightly increased” and “slightly reduced.” They do not publish DPS tables, HP totals, or stealth timers. Community damage spreadsheets with no patch date are not official. Deluxe Fate Coins buy cosmetics. They do not buy a better class.

Illegal Teaming is a real ban category, but it means pre-made squads who entered the same match to ruin others. Ordinary in-match deals are not that rule. There are no official class codes, extract tickets, or stance unlock strings.

If a page sells you an official ranking, it is inventing a document Bellring has not published. Confirm the kit in your skill screen, then extract once on a cheap Hallowgrove loadout. If you cannot leave, the class is too expensive for you right now, regardless of anyone’s letters.
