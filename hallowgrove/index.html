Mistfall Hunter Hallowgrove: Forest Hunt Guide

Mistfall Hunter Hallowgrove is the bleached forest map where Soul Harvest can trigger. Learn what official notes confirm about its mist, bosses, and extract.

Mistfall Hunter Hallowgrove is one of the two live extraction maps, the bleached forest counterpart to Brandrgarde’s fortress. Official marketing places both regions in Weavereach. Official patches confirm that Hallowgrove has damaging Gyldenmist, that Soul Harvest can trigger there, and that a Returning Chime exists as a minimap object. That is the sourced map.

This page is a mistfall hunter hallowgrove guide for people who want the forest match without a fake street map. It will not name unsourced landmarks, invent circle timers, or list redeem codes. There are none. Fan POI tours are mostly 待确认.

What official copy actually calls Hallowgrove

Xbox Wire, written by Bellring’s senior marketing manager, is the cleanest official postcard. Weavereach spans “from the fortress of Brandrgarde to the bleached Hallowgrove.” Humans, Velgr, and Lizardfolk live in that shattered world. The line matters because it treats Hallowgrove as a place with a look and a culture, not a reskin of Brandrgarde.

Steam news then treats Hallowgrove as a current queue, not a beta leftover. August 6 fixed a bug where Hallowgrove’s Gyldenmist dealt no damage under specific circumstances. That sentence names the map and confirms the mist on that map is supposed to hurt you. August 12 later names Hallowgrove again as one of the two maps where Soul Harvest can trigger.

The official website does not split extract rules by map. It gives one world rule: defeat a Returner Woodling, obtain a Soul of Return, and find your way home. Read Hallowgrove through that rule first. Local objects such as the Returning Chime sit on top of it.

How Hallowgrove fits Season 1

Season 1 is Soul Hunt. Soul Harvest is the season ritual that can appear inside ordinary Hallowgrove matches. When it triggers, Soul Incubators, Soul Cocoons, and a Fate Altar spawn, and cocoon holders reveal one another. Higher Gyldening Intensity raises the chance.

Official notes do not publish a Hallowgrove-only percent. If you want more rituals, raise intensity and accept a harder forest. Hallowgrove is also old enough to have its own launch-week bugs. Steam mentions a Cursed Moonwane encounter with skills and a room, plus side tasks and a Gyldening progress bar.

Those notes prove the forest has scripted fights and seasonal UI. They do not prove a complete public boss list. Game Rant’s roadmap recap says Phase 1 unlocks one Mist Lord challenge in Hallowgrove and two in Brandrgarde. That split is press reporting of a roadmap image, so treat the exact boss count as unconfirmed — check official notes.

Einherjar mode is live as of the August 6 update. Dried Flower Knots drop, and you can challenge Mist Lords in that mode. Official news does not name the Hallowgrove lord in that sentence, so do not invent one. If the in-match altar is present, the tutorial on that altar is the source of truth.

Extract on Hallowgrove, without the fan-fiction split

Public guides argue that Hallowgrove extract is a Returning Chime you activate, while Brandrgarde extract is a roam for Woodlings. Official public pages do not reprint that full contrast. They do confirm the Returning Chime by name, because August 12 fixed its minimap unlock status. They also confirm Soul Trees and extraction points, because launch notes talk about summoning an extraction point at a Soul Tree and spending a Soul of Return.

The safe reading is simple. In Hallowgrove you still need the official exit items: Returner Woodling, Soul of Return, Soul Tree, and extraction point. If a Returning Chime is unlocked on your minimap, it is an official object you can interact with. Whether every forest extract must start there is 待确认.

Check the in-game tutorial for Escape or Returning Chime after you load in. Do not route a first Hallowgrove run around Smuggler Woodling, Soul Ferry, or named groves that only exist on fan maps. Those names are 待确认. Brandrgarde’s Mine Pit is named in a Steam collision fix, which is why this page will name that Brandrgarde spot and still refuse to invent a Hallowgrove street grid.

Xbox Wire’s extract image is the same on every map. You ring the bell and step into the portal. Combined with the official site, the bell is the presentation of the exit, not a replacement for Soul of Return.

How the forest should change your decisions

Hallowgrove’s mist is a timer you cannot see in official numbers. Steam only confirms that the Gyldenmist there deals damage when it is working correctly. Community pages describe a late, sudden collapse instead of a shrinking circle. That contrast is not in the Steam posts fetched for this guide, so it stays unconfirmed — check official notes.

Play as if the mist can close, because the damage bug fix would be meaningless if the forest mist were cosmetic. Other Gyldhunters will use the same forest. Official site copy already tells you that hunting hunters is a faster loot route, and that death costs everything. A three-player squad is the official recommendation for large monsters and safe extracts.

Solo is allowed. Brandrgarde Solo Mode will not help you here, and it is not live anywhere yet. It is a September mid-season plan for Brandrgarde Normal and Cataclysm only. Soul Harvest makes Hallowgrove louder, because a Fate Altar and resonating cocoons pull people toward the same clearings you might have wanted for a quiet Woodling.

If your goal is a first extract, you can walk past incubators. If your goal is Mistchaser Shop tokens, you came to the right map, but you still need the Woodling afterward. Official rewards for the ritual include Soulbound Serpent Crystal, material chests, Moonlight Nectar, and Soul Coins. Official notes do not say those items drop from random forest chests.

Richie can appear in Hallowgrove matches the same way he appears in Brandrgarde matches. August 12 lets you spend one Soul Cocoon on him for a team reward, and the same patch fixed the merchant trapping people in corners. He is a shop, not an extract.

What to run, and what to wait for

For a new Gyldhunter, Hallowgrove is the map to learn stance swaps and the Woodling. Press consensus still points at Mercenary for that job. Official notes point at a Training Room if you want to practice before the forest. Take a cheap loadout, bank the first successful haul, then decide whether a harder Hallowgrove tier or Brandrgarde is next.

Official news does not publish the clearance numbers for those tiers in the posts used here. 待确认 — read the in-client map panel. Wait on mid-season forest promises as well. Game Rant’s roadmap recap lists an Ancestor Tree random match event later in Season 1, but the fetched August 5 Steam text does not transcribe a Hallowgrove-only Ancestor Tree line.

Until Bellring writes that event into a patch note, treat it as upcoming, not live. There are no official Hallowgrove codes, extract tickets, or mist-skip items sold as redeem strings. Deluxe Fate Coins are cosmetics, and Soul Coins are a named in-game currency. Neither opens a hidden forest door.
