Mistfall Hunter Mercenary: Starter Class Guide

Mistfall Hunter Mercenary is the press starter class. Official notes confirm Sword and Shield; treat hammer claims and HP ranks as unconfirmed.

Mistfall Hunter Mercenary is the class most launch-week press tells a new Gyldhunter to pick. Game Rant’s 29 July 2026 ranking calls it the best overall starting class because the kit survives bad fights while you learn extracts. That is press consensus, not a Bellring letter rank. Official materials only promise six classes, two stances each, and a live balance process.

This mistfall hunter mercenary guide keeps the official facts narrow and the press claims labeled. It will not invent damage numbers, a hidden stance unlock, or a redeem code. There are no official Mercenary codes.

What official notes actually confirm

Steam news is the safest Mercenary document. The August 6 live update names Mercenary Sword and Shield and fixes an auto-block that could trigger under specific circumstances. That sentence does two jobs. It proves Sword and Shield is a real official stance, and it proves beginners should expect that stance to block when they ask it to, not when the client decides.

The August 12 live update then leaves Mercenary untouched. Five of the six official classes received balance lines that day. Mercenary did not. Bellring’s own August 6 warning still applies: a class missing from a patch is under review, not crowned. Do not read silence as an official buff.

The Steam store never names Mercenary in the storefront blurb. It only promises six distinct classes and two unique weapon stances you can swap mid-battle. Talent trees, gem affixes, and active skills let you lean into assault, maneuvering, crowd control, or defense. Mercenary is one of those six because later patch notes say so.

What press adds, and what it does not prove

Game Rant describes Mercenary as a melee fighter who can move between a huge hammer and a sword and shield. Versatility is the pitch. The same article calls it the best class for solo players because of survivability, mobility, and durability. It also claims the highest HP in the game, the highest damage reduction, and letter grades of S in solo, A in trios, and B in PvP.

Those judgments, including any letter grades and the “highest HP” line, are Game Rant’s. Official notes never publish an HP table, a damage-reduction rank, or a solo-versus-trio score. Treat the hammer as press-reported until you confirm it in the skill screen. Official store text only promises two stances per class.

Prima Games’ recap of the August 6 patch repeats the official Sword and Shield auto-block fix and nothing else. That is the right amount of extra detail. If a later community build guide names a full hammer rotation, check the live client. Skill names that never appear in Steam news stay 待确认.

Why Mercenary is still a good first hunt

Extraction punishes deaths, not low highlight damage. Official copy never gives Mercenary the highest HP. The useful official fact is that you can swap stances and still play the same Gyldhunter. A first extract is a Returner Woodling, a Soul of Return, and a Soul Tree, not a boss time trial.

Mercenary is a good first main because the stance Steam already named is a defensive one. Sword and Shield is the kit you will actually learn while Hallowgrove teaches dodge, breakaway, and when to leave. Use the Training Room if the menus offer it. Launch notes mention public Training Room settings, which means the mode exists.

You can hunt solo or in a three-player squad. Steam says fighting back-to-back is the surest way to crush large threats and extract. Game Rant’s solo-friendly read is still only press. Official notes do not publish a Mercenary-only solo multiplier. August 12 did cut Blackarrow and Sorcerer solo PvP damage. Mercenary was not in that sentence.

Brandrgarde Solo Mode will not change this tomorrow. Bellring scheduled that queue for Normal and Cataclysm in the middle of Season 1 in September. Until that patch ships, do not assume a dedicated Mercenary solo playlist exists.

How to play Mercenary without a fake build

Open the skill screen and confirm both stances before you spend a warehouse on one identity. Official design wants the swap. If Sword and Shield is the only stance you can prove from Steam, learn that one first. If a hammer line is present in your client, it is real for you even if this page still marks community hammer names 待确认.

Lock Pip’s Pouches and Keychains before you queue. Steam patched players who tried to change them after matchmaking started. Official news never published a “safe” Mercenary gear budget, so if you cannot replace an item, do not take it into the mist.

Soul Harvest can trigger in ordinary Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches. Official notes do not assign Mercenary to the Fate Altar or the Soulgnawer. Bring the kit you can extract with. The ritual still sits inside a match you can fail.

Ignore anyone selling an official Mercenary ranking, a damage spreadsheet with no patch date, or a redeem-code reroll. Deluxe Fate Coins buy cosmetics. They do not buy a better Mercenary. There are no official class codes.

Common Mercenary mistakes

Do not treat Game Rant’s S-tier solo score as a Bellring document. Do not wait for a hammer patch that official notes have not written. Do not skip the extract because a seasonal altar looks more important. The store’s win condition is still a full haul returned to camp.

Do not invent Mercenary numbers. Official patches speak in “slightly increased” and “slightly reduced.” They have not published Mercenary HP, block windows, or hammer multipliers in the posts used here. 待确认 — check the in-game tutorial and the skill screen.
