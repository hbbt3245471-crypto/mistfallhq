Mistfall Hunter How to Extract: Soul of Return

Learn how to extract in Mistfall Hunter: defeat a Returner Woodling, claim a Soul of Return, then summon an extraction point at a Soul Tree before you die.

To extract in Mistfall Hunter you must earn a way home, not walk to a fixed door. Official materials say you hunt a Returner Woodling, take a Soul of Return, and use that item at a Soul Tree to summon an extraction point. Die before you leave and you lose the spoils you are carrying. That is the hunt.

This page answers mistfall hunter how to extract from official pages only. Public guides disagree about bells, chimes, and map-specific exits. Claims missing from those pages are marked unconfirmed.

The official extract loop

Mistfall Hunter is a third-person PvPvE extraction ARPG. You drop into Gyldenmist-choked ruins as a Gyldhunter, fight Corroded creatures and rival hunters, and try to leave with Gyldenblood. The Steam store is blunt: death strips you of all your spoils. The official site is equally blunt about the exit.

You locate a rare monster called the Returner Woodling, defeat it, and obtain a Soul of Return, “with which you may find your way home.” The publisher launch release repeats that beat in plainer language: reach a Returner Woodling and you extract with your haul intact. Fall, and it is all left behind. You can hunt solo or in a three-player squad.

Launch-week Steam notes name the last mechanical step. Players summon an extraction point around a Soul Tree, and that summon consumes a Soul of Return. A launch bug even punished people who walked in and out of the Soul Tree area while summoning, because the game could spend an extra soul. That report confirms the official objects: Soul Tree, extraction point, and Soul of Return.

Bell versus Soul of Return

Some community writeups treat a “bell” as the extract item itself. Official copy does not support replacing Soul of Return with a lootable bell. The official website names Soul of Return as the item you get from the Woodling. Steam’s July 29 and July 30 notes name it again as the item spent when you summon an extraction point.

Bellring’s Xbox Wire launch article does mention a bell, but it places that bell at the portal moment. The marketing line is that pressure breaks when you “ring the bell and step into the extraction portal.” Read together, official sources support Soul of Return as the required item and a bell as part of how the exit is presented. They do not support a separate official extract currency called a bell.

If a guide tells you to “loot the bell and leave,” treat that as community shorthand. Check your inventory for a Soul of Return before you commit to the Soul Tree. If the item is not there, you are not ready to summon.

Hallowgrove chime versus Brandrgarde roam

The second public conflict is map-specific. Many fan pages say Hallowgrove extract starts at a Returning Chime, while Brandrgarde extract is a roam for Woodlings. Official public pages do not republish that full flowchart. They do confirm the named pieces.

Steam’s August 12 live update fixed a minimap bug for the unlock status of the Returning Chime. That is enough to treat Returning Chime as a real in-match object. The official site and Steam store still describe the Returner Woodling as the monster you must reach. Soul Harvest notes also treat Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde as the two live maps where seasonal gameplay can trigger.

Official news does not say every Hallowgrove exit must start at a chime, or that every Brandrgarde exit is a random roam. How a clean extract actually feels

Start the match with a reason to leave. The Steam store loop is scavenge, fight, and extract. Official copy never publishes a safe loot timer or a Soul Tree lifetime, so those numbers stay unconfirmed — check official notes. Leave when you have a Soul of Return and a path to a Soul Tree.

Keep the Woodling fight short enough that you can still move. Publisher and store text both frame the Woodling as the gate to camp, which means other Gyldhunters hear the same objective. Steam says a three-player squad is the surest way to crush large threats and extract. Solo is also official.

When you reach the Soul Tree, summon once and stay in the area. Launch notes exist because leaving and re-entering while summoning could burn a second Soul of Return. After the extraction point appears, Xbox Wire’s last official image is to ring the bell and step into the portal. Exact ring counts and wither timers are 待确认.

Bank what you bring back before you queue again. Official posts mention a warehouse, loadouts, and Pip’s Pouches. They do not publish a complete stash tutorial in news posts. Do not re-enter still carrying gear you cannot replace.

What can stop you from leaving

The Gyldenmist is not flavor text. Steam patched a bug where Hallowgrove’s Gyldenmist dealt no damage, which confirms the mist is a damaging field on that map. Brandrgarde has its own mist pressure and a Cataclysm difficulty. Official notes give no damage figure, so treat the mist as lethal.

Rival Gyldhunters are the other official threat. The website says you can hunt other hunters for a quicker haul, and that death costs everything. Illegal Teaming means players who deliberately enter the same match to work together against others. Ordinary in-match deals are not that rule.

Soul Harvest can also steal the extract. It can trigger inside ordinary Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches, spawning Soul Incubators, Soul Cocoons, and a Fate Altar. Cocoon holders reveal each other. If you came for a quiet first extract, ignore the altar and still hunt a Woodling.

Practical extract checklist

Treat this as a reading of official steps, not a hidden mechanic list. The objects below are the ones Steam, the official site, and Xbox Wire actually name. Timers and extra exits stay unconfirmed.

  1. Queue solo or as a trio into Hallowgrove or Brandrgarde.
  2. Loot lightly enough that a death does not wipe a stash you cannot rebuild.
  3. Find and defeat a Returner Woodling.
  4. Confirm a Soul of Return is actually in your possession.
  5. Reach a Soul Tree and summon the extraction point without bouncing in and out of the area.
  6. Use the portal. Official marketing describes ringing a bell as you step through.

Brandrgarde Solo Mode is not a live extract rule. Bellring said it will arrive for Normal and Cataclysm in the middle of Season 1 in September. Until that patch ships, do not assume a dedicated solo queue exists. There are also no official redeem codes.
