# Mistfall Hunter Camp Upgrades: Official Systems Only

Mistfall Hunter camp upgrades are real, but most public tables are not. Official materials confirm that a successful extract returns you to the safety of your camp, and Steam news then names a warehouse you can upgrade, a Soulcatch Tree that uses Soulcatch Nets, an Auction House, a Loadout screen, and a Collection Pedestal for displaying loot. That is the sourced hub.

This mistfall hunter camp upgrades page will not print community material lists, slot bonuses, or a building order that Bellring has not published. It will not treat a marketing camp name as a confirmed proper noun. There are no official upgrade codes.

## What official copy actually calls the hub

The Steam store is the safest sentence. Death strips you of all your spoils. To return to the safety of your camp, you must carve a path to a Returner Woodling. Official website copy is the same loop without a hub name: escape with your spoils and find your way home.

Xbox Wire, written by Bellring’s senior marketing manager, is the one official page that uses a camp name. It says the pressure melts when you ring the bell, step into the extraction portal, and return safely to Windrest camp. That is marketing copy from a Bellring manager, not a Steam systems post. This guide will not state Windrest as fact. Treat the proper name as 待确认 and use the store’s word: camp.

The July 17 Official Launch Announcement is the other official camp sentence. Among launch features it lists “Collection Pedestal: Display your hard-earned loot right in camp.” The same FAQ block, as reprinted in Steam announcement indexes, says camp functionality will be progressively expanded with future seasonal updates. Official posts used here do not publish a complete building roster.

## Systems Steam news actually names

Warehouse is the official stash. August 12 fixed warehouse items that occasionally disappeared from view while you prepared gear out of a match. The same patch fixed a visual UI display issue when upgrading the warehouse to Level 13. That is enough to treat the warehouse as an upgradable camp system that reaches at least Level 13. Official notes do not publish slot counts, level caps, or the material cost of any warehouse tier.

Soulcatch Tree is a separate camp object from the in-match Soul Tree. August 6 fixed missing localization text for certain parts of the Soulcatch Tree. August 12 fixed a bug where the Soulcatch Tree had a small chance to yield Holy materials even when it was not equipped with a Full Moon Soulcatch Net. Those two lines confirm the tree, the nets, and a Full Moon net as official. They do not confirm a production timer or a drop table. Community “money tree” writeups stay 待确认.

Auction House is official. Launch-week notes fixed controller gem-slot selection there. August 12 fixed the attribute panel when you put items up for sale, listing prices that failed to refresh, and a Loadout bug that purchased gems from the Auction House even when those gems were already in the warehouse. Official notes do not publish how many listing slots an upgrade adds.

Loadout is the official out-of-match gear screen. July 30 fixed unequipping equipment from the Loadout system on a controller. August 12 tied that screen to the warehouse and the Auction House. Official news never published a complete stash tutorial.

Pip’s Pouches and Keychains sit next to camp prep even if they are not a building. August 6 notes, in the previously-fixed list, stop players from changing Pip’s Pouches and Keychains after matchmaking has started. August 1 mail included a Pip’s Rare Pouch Voucher. Lock the bag before you queue.

## Collection Pedestal, Fate Chart, and other hub UI

Collection Pedestal is the one camp feature the July 17 announcement describes in plain language: display hard-earned loot right in camp. Official public posts used here do not say it grants combat power. That matches the store’s no-pay-to-win rule. Fate Coins buy cosmetics. They do not buy warehouse space.

Fate Chart is the seasonal task board, not a camp building, but August 12 fixed a hotkey that could not switch to the Season tab while you were inside the warehouse interface. Contracts share the same weekly-refresh bug list. Path of Glory appears on the settlement screen. Training Room settings exist. None of those systems replace the extract.

Richie is an in-match merchant, not a camp vendor. Do not confuse him with hub shops that only appear in community guides.

## What community upgrade tables invent

Fan pages now list seven, nine, or ten camp buildings, complete with Pearl Dust costs and “+24 storage” bonuses. Those tables disagree with each other. Official Steam posts fetched for this guide do not name Goddess Statue, Workshop, Tavern, Scavenger Squad, Cazarro’s Shop, or a Gyldenblood Pool as upgrade stations. They also do not publish a player-level gate or a Gyldenblood unlock threshold. All of that is 待确认 — read the in-client camp menu.

Because those names are unsourced here, this page will not rank them. The only upgrade Steam has proven with a level number is the warehouse at Level 13. The only camp showcase Steam has named in a launch bullet is the Collection Pedestal. The only passive camp object Steam has tied to a net is the Soulcatch Tree.

If your client shows more stations than this page lists, trust the client. This fan guide is not a replacement for that UI. Screenshot the upgrade panel after a patch, because launch-week notes already prove warehouse and Soulcatch Tree text can be wrong.

## How to spend a return without a fake priority list

Bank first. Official copy is blunt: death costs everything you are carrying, and the win condition is a full haul returned to camp. Move loot into the warehouse before you queue again. Official notes do not say extracted items auto-bank.

Then open the camp menu the client actually gives you. If a warehouse upgrade is available, that is the one official notes have already treated as a leveled system. If a Soulcatch Net slot is empty, the August 12 bug fix is your reminder that the net type matters. If the Collection Pedestal is still locked, the July 17 bullet still only promises a display, not a stat.

Do not spend a seasonal token on a vanity station while you still cannot store the next extract. Do not buy Deluxe Fate Coins to skip a building. Official messaging is zero pay-to-win and no paid stat boosts. There are no redeem codes for camp materials.

Brandrgarde Solo Mode will not change the hub. It is a September mid-season plan for Brandrgarde Normal and Cataclysm, not a camp unlock. Soul Harvest tokens belong in the Mistchaser Shop and the Fate Chart Shop, not in an invented upgrade recipe.
