# Mistfall Hunter Best Class for New Players

The honest mistfall hunter best class answer for a new player is Mercenary. That is press consensus, not a Bellring tier list. Game Rant’s launch-day ranking calls Mercenary the best overall starting class because it survives bad fights while you learn extracts. Official materials only promise six classes, two stances each, and a live balance process.

If you already know the extract loop and want a specialist, the same press piece still likes Withered Knight, Blackarrow, and Shadowstrix for different jobs. Those opinions move when patches land. Read them as community snapshots, then confirm the kit in your skill screen. Bellring has not published letter ranks.

## What official sources actually say about classes

The Steam store is the baseline. Six distinct classes define your path, and each class masters two unique weapon stances. You can swap fighting styles mid-battle. Talent trees, gem affixes, and active skills let you lean into assault, maneuvering, crowd control, or defense.

The store blurb does not name the six classes, but Steam news since launch does. The six official names in patch notes are Mercenary, Blackarrow, Shadowstrix, Sorcerer, Seer, and Withered Knight. Xbox Wire repeats the “six distinctive classes” line. The official site tells you to select a class, use magic and steel, and switch weapons during battles.

Bellring also keeps saying balance is unfinished. The August 6 notes warn that a class missing from a patch is still under review, not “perfect.” The August 12 notes say the team is monitoring the meta, including areas not covered that day. Any webpage that sells you a frozen official ranking is inventing a document Bellring has not published.

## Mercenary: the press starter

Game Rant describes Mercenary as a melee fighter who can move between a huge hammer and a sword and shield. Versatility is the pitch. The same article calls it the best class for solo players because of survivability, mobility, and durability. Those judgments, including any letter grades, are Game Rant’s, not Bellring’s.

What official notes confirm is narrower and safer. Mercenary Sword and Shield exists, and August 6 fixed an auto-block bug on that stance. That matters for beginners because the stance you will actually learn first is the one the patch already names. Hammer details in press and community guides should be checked in-game before you build a whole identity around them.

Mercenary is a good first main because extraction punishes deaths, not low highlight damage. Official copy never gives Mercenary the highest HP in the game. Game Rant does, so treat the “highest HP” claim as press opinion. The useful official fact is that you can swap stances and still play the same Gyldhunter.

## The other five, without fake tiers

Withered Knight is the other frontline official class. Launch notes buffed Polearm and Shield: cheaper Charged Dash energy, better low-tier turning, stronger basic thrusts, a stronger Spear Barrage multiplier, cheaper Rainbow Piercer, and cheaper Block. The launch release framed that archetype as a new shield-and-spear line built for balanced attack and defense. Game Rant still talks about a two-hander and a Wither debuff, so confirm both stances in the skill screen.

Blackarrow is the official bow class. August 6 removed impact from uncharged Steel Arrow shots and reduced impact on full charges. August 12 then lengthened several arrow cooldowns, shortened Sonic Arrow, and reduced Blackarrow’s PvP damage multiplier in solo mode while leaving PvE damage unchanged. Official balance now treats solo PvP as a different number from PvE.

Sorcerer is the official spell class. August 12 retuned Fire Bolt, Flameblade, Crystal Icebolt, Deep Freeze, and Thunderstrike, and it also cut Sorcerer’s solo PvP damage multiplier. Launch notes mention a Stardust playstyle and a Chant Guard talent for Stardust spells. If you want a first character, that PvP cut is a warning, not a dare.

Shadowstrix is the official ambush class. August 12 touched Spinning Slash, Sleight of Hand, Phantom Shift, Battle Frenzy, Inspiring Impale, Wound Accumulation, and Aftereffect. Game Rant calls it the mobility and single-target specialist with stealth. Official notes do not publish a stealth duration or a burst multiplier, but they do show a class that received a lot of live tuning.

Seer is the official support-leaning class. August 12 fixed a Reverent Seer Punishment Rune that could lock onto Richie the merchant, which at least confirms a Reverent path and rune tools. Game Rant describes life-and-death magic, totems, runes, and sigils. Official public notes do not rank Seer for solo play, so it is a press-shaped trio pick rather than the consensus first solo class.

## Best class depends on the question you asked

Best class to learn the game: Mercenary, per press consensus. Best class to hold a trio is a community argument, and official notes only say a three-player squad is the surest way to crush large threats and extract. Best class after the August 12 solo PvP multiplier cuts is unconfirmed — check official notes and your own matches.

Best class for Soul Harvest is also unconfirmed. The ritual can trigger in Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde, cocoon holders reveal each other, and the Soulgnawer is the named seasonal threat. Official notes do not assign a class to that fight. Bring the kit you can extract with, because the ritual still sits inside a match you can fail.

Best class for Brandrgarde-Cataclysm is unconfirmed. The launch release only calls that difficulty punishing, with its own missions and rewards. Best class for Brandrgarde Solo Mode is not a live question, because that queue is scheduled for mid Season 1 in September.

## How to choose without a tier list

Open the class screen and look for two stances you are willing to practice. Official design wants you to swap mid-fight, so a class whose second stance bores you is a worse pick than a “lower tier” class you will actually swap on. Then play the Training Room and one cheap Hallowgrove run. If you cannot extract, the class is too expensive for you right now, regardless of Game Rant’s letters.

Watch what Bellring is actually tuning. A class that just took a solo PvP multiplier cut is not secretly the new official best. A class that only received bug fixes may still be strong, or it may simply be later in the review queue. The August 6 warning is the most useful official sentence on this topic: missing from a patch does not mean perfect.

Ignore anyone selling an official ranking, a damage spreadsheet with no patch date, or a redeem-code reroll. There are no official class codes. Deluxe Fate Coins buy cosmetics. They do not buy a better Mercenary.
