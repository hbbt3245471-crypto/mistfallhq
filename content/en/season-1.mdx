# Mistfall Hunter Season 1 Soul Hunt: Live Guide

Mistfall Hunter Season 1 is officially named Soul Hunt. The July 17 Steam Official Launch Announcement and the July 30 Games Press release both use that title. It is not a post-launch surprise. Season 1 shipped with the client on 29 July 2026 at 6:00 PM PT (01:00 UTC on 30 July) on Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox, including Day One Xbox Game Pass.

Soul Hunt is the season. Soul Harvest is the season-exclusive ritual inside it. Those are two different official bullets. This mistfall hunter season 1 guide keeps them apart and stays inside Steam news, the launch release, and Xbox Wire. It will not invent Battle Pass tier counts, drop rates, or redeem codes. There are none.

## Soul Hunt is the season name

Games Press is the cleanest public story. Working alongside Dew, hunters set out to thwart the Varg clan’s attempt to forge a false god, tracking soul-warped creatures and returning lost souls to the Web of Fate. Steam’s August 12 overview uses a later wording: Gyldhunters must thwart the Velgr’s attempts to create a False God. Use Velgr inside the current patch. Varg is the launch-release clan name.

Xbox Wire explains why a season exists at all. Successful extracts mend the shattered Web of Fate and push the world’s story forward. Each season introduces fresh narrative updates, main quests, and limited-time events. The battlefield mutates with new classes, uncharted zones, and relics. Games Press adds the live-service clock: new seasons roughly every three months, with no pay-to-win and no purchasable stat boosts.

The July 17 launch announcement listed Season 1 content as its own block: Soul Hunt seasonal tasks and events, Soul Harvest themed gameplay, Holy weapons and exclusive affixes, Brandrgarde-Cataclysm, a Collection Pedestal in camp, a Ladder and Tier Reward system, more cosmetics, and a Battle Pass that “will be completely free.”

## What is live as of 15 August 2026

Soul Harvest is live. The launch release told press it would go live on August 14. That date is now outdated. Steam’s August 7 notice said the next week’s client update would officially launch Soul Harvest and open Season Tasks. The August 12 live update then shipped the gameplay at 08:00 UTC+0, alongside a new stage of the Fate Chart.

A later official post, “Server Stutter Optimization Update & Soul Harvest Launch Rewards,” speaks in the present tense. Soul Harvest is now underway. Complete runs to earn exclusive tokens, then exchange them at the Mistchaser Shop for Soulbound Serpent Crystal, a Holy Material Selection Chest, Moonlight Nectar, and Soul Coins. The Fate Chart Shop also gained Moonlight Nectar and a Legendary Material Selection Chest.

Einherjar mode is live as of the August 6 update. Dried Flower Knots drop, and you can challenge Mist Lords in that mode. The August 1 “Second Wave” post said defeating the Mist Lord grants a higher chance of dropping Holy Weapons or Holy Forging Materials. Official posts never published that chance as a number.

Fate Chart is the seasonal task board. Steam has patched weekly refresh times, Selection Chest displays, and a countdown timer. Path of Glory appears in settlement-screen notes when points are deducted. The July 17 announcement called the same idea a Ladder and Tier Reward system. Exact tier names and unlock levels from community recaps of Developer Update #7 stay 待确认 unless you can read them in-client.

The free Season 1 track is the Slumbering Contract. Games Press promised a free seven-day login event that unlocks it at no cost. The July 17 announcement said the Season 1 Battle Pass is completely free. Official public posts used here do not reprint a full reward table.

## What is scheduled, not shipped

Brandrgarde Solo Mode is not live. Bellring’s 5 August post confirmed it for Normal and Cataclysm in the middle of Season 1 in September. The studio also said two new queues will split matchmaking and create solo balance problems it has not finished solving. No official post has named a patch day.

Game Rant’s 12 August roadmap recap, written from Bellring’s roadmap image, splits the rest of Season 1 into later phases. Phase 2 in that recap includes fully unlocked season tasks, an S1 Pip’s Rare Pouch, a Nether Ferry skin, Brandrgarde Solo Mode, an Underlord Speed Challenge, and bonus rewards from Fern the Woodling. Phase 3 adds a Golden Woodling, an Ancestor Tree random match event, and Season 2 clues. Those names come from press reporting of a graphic, not from a Steam changelog. Treat anything not yet in a patch note as upcoming — check official notes.

The same recap teases Season 2 with a new Blackarrow Javelin and a new seasonal progression system. The July 17 announcement had already said Blackarrow’s new weapon archetype would launch in a future season. That is still one of the six classes, not a seventh.

## How to play the season without throwing the extract

Soul Hunt sits on top of the normal extract loop. You still need a Returner Woodling and a Soul of Return if you want to leave with your haul. Soul Harvest can trigger inside ordinary Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches. Higher Gyldening Intensity raises the chance. Official notes do not publish percentages.

If a Fate Altar or a Soul Cocoon appears, you can ignore it and still leave through a Woodling. If you came for Mistchaser Shop tokens, you still need the extract afterward. Official notes point at a boss named Soulgnawer in the launch-rewards post. The launch release had previewed a shapeshifting elite, the Soul-Gnawer. Exact fight phases are unconfirmed — check official notes and the in-game tutorial.

Fair play is not optional flavor. Bellring publishes regular penalty lists for cheating and Illegal Teaming. Report suspects in-game. There are no official redeem codes. Official rewards for this window are in-game mail, the Mistchaser Shop, the Fate Chart Shop, and the free Slumbering Contract.

Login mail is separate from ritual tokens. Bellring has sent Soul Coins, combat bags, Moonlight Nectar, and cosmetic poses through dated mail windows. Those windows close. They are thank-yous, not drop tables.

## What this page will not invent

No Battle Pass XP curve, no Path of Glory bracket list, no Soul Harvest token-per-run value, and no Cataclysm clearance number. Official patches speak in “slightly increased” and present-tense shop lists. Community roadmaps that merge Soul Hunt and Soul Harvest into one event name are mixing two Steam bullets.

If a third-party article still headlines “August 14 BIG Update,” it is recycling the July 30 press date. If a page sells Season 1 codes, it is inventing a system Bellring has not published. Deluxe Fate Coins remain cosmetics. Soul Coins are a named in-game currency. Neither is a pay-to-win stat boost.
