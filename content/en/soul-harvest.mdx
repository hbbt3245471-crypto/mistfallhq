# Mistfall Hunter Soul Harvest: Live Ritual Guide

Mistfall Hunter Soul Harvest is live. It is not a future August 14 event and it is not a separate queue. Bellring’s August 12 live update enabled the ritual at 08:00 UTC+0 as a no-downtime client patch, and later Steam news treats the mode as already underway. You still play ordinary matches on Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde.

When the mechanic triggers, you gather Soul Cocoons, decide whether to feed Richie or the Fate Altar, and try to finish a purification prayer without dying. This mistfall hunter soul harvest guide is built from Steam news and the publisher launch release. It does not invent drop rates or a codes section.

## Soul Harvest is live, not “coming August 14”

The publisher launch release, dated 30 July 2026, told press that Soul Harvest would go live on August 14. That date is now outdated. Steam’s August 7 notice said the next week’s client update would officially launch Soul Harvest. The August 12 live update then shipped the gameplay at 08:00 UTC+0.

A later official post, “Server Stutter Optimization Update & Soul Harvest Launch Rewards,” speaks in the present tense. Soul Harvest is now underway. Complete runs to earn exclusive tokens, then exchange them at the Mistchaser Shop. If you are still waiting for August 14, you are reading the launch press release instead of the Steam patch that enabled the mode.

Season 1 itself is still called Soul Hunt. Soul Harvest is the season-exclusive ritual inside that season, not a replacement name for the whole live-service period. Steam’s August 12 overview calls it a special operation launched by Dew and the Gyldhunters to mend the Web of Fate.

## What the ritual is trying to do

Official flavor is unusually specific. Gyldhunters must thwart the Velgr’s attempts to create a False God, defeat mutated creatures warped by Soul Cocoons, and perform a purification prayer that returns disordered souls to the Web of Fate. The launch press release told a parallel Season 1 story about working with Dew against a clan trying to forge a false god. Steam’s live notes use the name Velgr, so use that wording inside the current patch.

The ritual is PvPvE by design. Other hunters can see the same incubators and the same altar. Anyone carrying a Soul Cocoon resonates with every other cocoon on the map. You can perceive those locations, and those holders can perceive you.

Bellring also points at a boss named Soulgnawer in the launch-rewards post. The team wishes Gyldhunters luck “defeating the Soulgnawer and claiming your Soul Harvest rewards.” The launch press release had already previewed a shapeshifting elite, the Soul-Gnawer, who stirs only for hunters who come prepared. Exact fight phases, health, and drop tables are unconfirmed — check official notes and the in-game tutorial.

## How Soul Harvest triggers in a match

Steam’s August 12 gameplay overview is the only official step list this guide will keep. Soul Harvest has to be active as a season mechanic, which it now is. During that window, Gyldhunters have a chance to trigger the gameplay in Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches. When it triggers, Soul Incubators carrying Soul Cocoons spawn at random, and a Fate Altar appears for the purification prayer.

Gyldening Intensity is the official dial. The higher the map’s Gyldening Intensity, the greater the chance of triggering Soul Harvest. Official news does not publish percentages for Normal, Chaos, or Brandrgarde-Cataclysm. 待确认 — do not treat a fan site’s percent as a Bellring number.

The altar does not open on spawn. After a match has been underway for a certain duration, hunters can submit two Soul Cocoons at the Fate Altar to activate it and begin the ritual. “A certain duration” is the official phrase, and no public Steam post gives the minute mark. Check the in-game tutorial if you need the timer.

Richie is an official side path. One Gyldhunter can submit one Soul Cocoon to Richie, the in-match merchant, to unlock a special Soul Cocoon reward for the whole team. That is a team reward, not a solo hidden stash. Official notes do not list what the reward roll contains, and Bellring ends the overview by pointing you at the in-game tutorial.

## Rewards you can actually name

The post-launch rewards note is the cleanest official shop list. Complete Soul Harvest runs to earn exclusive tokens. Exchange those tokens at the Mistchaser Shop for rare rewards, including a Season-exclusive Holy-quality collectible called Soulbound Serpent Crystal, a Holy Material Selection Chest, Moonlight Nectar, and Soul Coins. The same post added Moonlight Nectar and a Legendary Material Selection Chest to the Fate Chart Shop.

Login mail is separate from ritual tokens. Bellring sent Moonlight Nectar x5, Pro Combat Bag x2, and Advanced Combat Bag x2, claimable via in-game mail if you log in before 22 August 2026, 00:00 UTC+0. That window is a thank-you, not a drop table. Official posts never published a token-per-run value.

Fate Coins remain the Deluxe cosmetic currency on the Steam store. Soul Coins are a separate named currency used in launch mail and shops. Neither is a pay-to-win stat boost.

## How to play the ritual without throwing the extract

Soul Harvest sits on top of the normal extract loop. You still need a Returner Woodling and a Soul of Return if you want to leave with your haul. The altar is a second objective that other people can steal, camp, or complete first. If your bag is already full of gear you cannot replace, finishing the extract is still the official win condition of the game.

Cocoon resonance is the main tactical fact official notes give you. Carrying a cocoon paints you for every other carrier. A trio can split the job: one hunter holds cocoons, one watches the altar, one keeps an extract route alive. Official copy already says fighting back-to-back in a three-player squad is the surest way to crush large threats and leave safely.

Richie is the lower-commitment official spend. One cocoon buys a team reward without opening the altar. Two cocoons at the Fate Altar start the ritual itself. Official news does not say you must do both, and it does not say the Richie turn-in blocks the altar.

Ignore community claims that Soul Harvest is its own playlist or a timed world event on a public clock. Steam names both Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde. It names chance, not a schedule. It names Gyldening Intensity, not a hidden server hour.

## What this page will not invent

No drop rates, Soulgnawer HP, incubator counts, or altar channel length. Brandrgarde Solo Mode is not live and is not required. Bellring scheduled that queue for Normal and Cataclysm in the middle of Season 1 in September.

There are no redeem codes. Official rewards for this window are in-game mail, the Mistchaser Shop, and the Fate Chart Shop. If a third-party article still headlines “August 14 BIG Update,” it is recycling the July 30 press date.
