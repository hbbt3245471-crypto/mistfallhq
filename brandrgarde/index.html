Mistfall Hunter Brandrgarde: Fortress Hunt Guide

Mistfall Hunter Brandrgarde is the fortress map with Cataclysm. Soul Harvest can trigger here. Solo Mode is not live until mid Season 1 in September.

Mistfall Hunter Brandrgarde is the fortress counterpart to the bleached Hallowgrove. Xbox Wire, written by Bellring’s senior marketing manager, places both regions in Weavereach: “from the fortress of Brandrgarde to the bleached Hallowgrove.” Official patches then treat Brandrgarde as a current queue with its own Cataclysm difficulty, a named Mine Pit, and a chance to trigger Soul Harvest. That is the sourced map.

This page is a mistfall hunter brandrgarde guide for people who want the fortress match without a fake street map. It will not name unsourced landmarks, invent circle timers, or list redeem codes. There are none. Fan POI tours are mostly 待确认.

What official copy actually calls Brandrgarde

Xbox Wire is the cleanest official postcard. Weavereach is a shattered world of humans, Velgr, and Lizardfolk. Brandrgarde is named as a fortress, not as a reskin of the forest. The official website does not split extract rules by map. It gives one world rule: defeat a Returner Woodling, obtain a Soul of Return, and find your way home.

Steam news then treats Brandrgarde as live content, not a beta leftover. August 6 fixed Allowed Combat Bags displaying incorrectly for Brandrgarde in Cataclysm mode. The same patch fixed players getting stuck after a dodge in Brandrgarde’s Mine Pit. Those two sentences are why this page will name Cataclysm and Mine Pit and still refuse to invent a fortress street grid.

The July 17 Official Launch Announcement and the July 30 Games Press release both add Brandrgarde-Cataclysm as a punishing new tier with its own missions and rewards. Cataclysm is a difficulty on Brandrgarde, not a third map. Official public posts used here do not publish clearance numbers or bag rules beyond the fact that Allowed Combat Bags exist as a UI field.

Solo Mode is not live

Bellring’s 5 August roadmap post is the line that matters most on this page. Since launch, players asked for a Solo Mode for Brandrgarde. After “intensive discussions and preliminary development,” the studio confirmed Solo Mode for both the Normal and Cataclysm difficulties of Brandrgarde in the middle of Season 1 in September.

That sentence entails the current state. A mode being introduced in September is a mode that is not in the live queue today. GamerFuzion reprints the same Steam wording. No official post has named a patch day. 待确认 — wait for the Steam note that actually ships it.

Bellring also flagged the cost. Adding two new modes will split the matchmaking queue and create fresh class balance challenges in solo play. The studio said it will evaluate those factors and communicate before further adjustments. Do not treat a fan site’s “solo Brandrgarde is open” claim as a Bellring queue.

You can already hunt solo in the general sense. The Steam store says you may stalk the mist as a lone wolf or band together in a three-player squad. That is not the same thing as a dedicated Brandrgarde Solo Mode.

Extract on Brandrgarde, without the fan-fiction split

Public guides argue that Hallowgrove extract starts at a Returning Chime, while Brandrgarde extract is a roam for Woodlings. Official public pages do not reprint that full contrast. They do confirm the named pieces.

The official site and Steam store still describe the Returner Woodling as the monster you must reach. Launch notes confirm Soul Trees and extraction points, because walking in and out of a Soul Tree area while summoning could spend an extra Soul of Return. Xbox Wire’s extract image is the same on every map: ring the bell and step into the portal.

Steam’s August 12 update fixed the minimap unlock status of the Returning Chime. That is enough to treat Returning Chime as a real in-match object. Official news does not say Brandrgarde has no chimes, or that every fortress exit is a random roam. 待确认 — check the in-game tutorial for the map you queued.

How Brandrgarde fits Season 1

Season 1 is Soul Hunt. Soul Harvest is the season ritual that can appear inside ordinary Brandrgarde matches as well as Hallowgrove matches. When it triggers, Soul Incubators, Soul Cocoons, and a Fate Altar spawn, and cocoon holders reveal one another. Higher Gyldening Intensity raises the chance. Official notes do not publish a Brandrgarde-only percent.

Richie can appear in Brandrgarde matches the same way he appears in Hallowgrove matches. August 12 lets you spend one Soul Cocoon on him for a team reward, and the same patch fixed the merchant trapping people in corners. He is a shop, not an extract.

Einherjar mode is live as of the August 6 update. Dried Flower Knots drop, and you can challenge Mist Lords in that mode. Game Rant’s roadmap recap says Phase 1 unlocks two Mist Lord challenges in Brandrgarde and one in Hallowgrove. That split is press reporting of a roadmap image, so treat the exact boss count as unconfirmed — check official notes. Official news does not name the Brandrgarde lord in the Dried Flower Knot sentence.

Main tasks that mention the fortress exist. August 12 updated completion conditions for “Mist Lord’s Power,” “Lay Them to Rest,” and “End the Cataclysm.” Those titles prove Cataclysm is a story beat, not only a queue label.

How the fortress should change your decisions

Play as if the mist can close. Official notes give no Brandrgarde damage figure, but Hallowgrove’s Gyldenmist is a damaging field when it works, and Brandrgarde has its own Cataclysm pressure. Other Gyldhunters will use the same fortress. Official site copy already tells you that hunting hunters is a faster loot route, and that death costs everything.

If your goal is a first extract, Brandrgarde-Cataclysm is the wrong classroom. Press consensus still points at Mercenary for learning stance swaps, and official notes point at a Training Room and a cheaper map first. Take a loadout you can replace, bank the haul, then decide whether Cataclysm is next.

Official news does not publish the clearance numbers for Normal or Cataclysm in the posts used here. 待确认 — read the in-client map panel. There are no official Brandrgarde codes, extract tickets, or mist-skip items sold as redeem strings. Deluxe Fate Coins are cosmetics. Soul Coins are a named in-game currency. Neither opens a hidden fortress door.
