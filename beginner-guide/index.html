Mistfall Hunter Beginner Guide: First Safe Hunt

A Mistfall Hunter beginner guide for launch-week Gyldhunters: pick Mercenary, learn the extract loop, and leave Hallowgrove with loot still in your bag.

A good first session in Mistfall Hunter is one successful extract, not a highlight-reel wipe. You pick a class, drop into the Gyldenmist, take only what you can afford to lose, defeat a Returner Woodling, and spend a Soul of Return at a Soul Tree so you can leave. Press coverage since launch treats Mercenary as the forgiving starter. Official materials treat the extract itself as the game.

This mistfall hunter beginner guide stays inside what the official site, Steam, Xbox Wire, and reputable press have actually published. It will not invent damage numbers, named shortcuts, or redeem codes. There are no official codes.

What you are actually playing

Mistfall Hunter is a dark fantasy extraction ARPG from Bellring Games, published with Skystone Games. It launched on 29 July 2026 at 6:00 PM PT (01:00 UTC on 30 July) on Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox, including Day One Xbox Game Pass. The Standard Edition is 24.99 US dollars on Steam. Deluxe is a cosmetic bundle on top of that, not a power purchase.

The story setup is consistent across official pages. The gods have fallen, and their blood became the Gyldenmist. A fragment of the Fate Goddess endures as Dew, who revives fallen heroes as Gyldhunters. Your job is to harvest Gyldenblood, mend the Web of Fate, and escape the ruins.

Xbox Wire names the shattered world Weavereach and frames every successful extract as narrative progress, not only personal loot. You can play alone or in a three-player squad. Six classes exist, and each one masters two weapon stances that you can swap in a fight. Official copy promises no pay-to-win and no paid stat boosts.

Pick a class you can survive

If you want the press consensus starter, pick Mercenary. Game Rant’s launch-day class article calls it the best overall starting class because the kit is durable, flexible, and less punishing while you learn extracts. That is community and press opinion, not an official tier list. Bellring has not published letter ranks.

Mercenary is still a real official class. Steam’s August 6 notes mention Mercenary Sword and Shield, including a fix for unwanted auto-blocks. Press describes a second hammer identity. Official store text only promises two stances per class, so treat the hammer as press-reported until you confirm it in the skill screen.

The other five official classes are Blackarrow, Shadowstrix, Sorcerer, Seer, and Withered Knight. Steam balance notes have touched all of them since launch. Withered Knight gained a Polearm and Shield archetype that the launch release called a new shield-and-spear line. None of those details make them better first characters than Mercenary for a new hunter.

Use the Training Room if the menus offer it. Launch notes mention public Training Room settings, which means the mode exists. Learn dodge, breakaway, and stance swaps there instead of inside a live extract.

Learn the extract before you learn the meta

The official site’s extract sentence should be your first-hour quest. Locate a Returner Woodling, defeat it, and obtain a Soul of Return. Steam launch notes add the last object: a Soul Tree where you summon an extraction point that consumes the Soul of Return. Xbox Wire adds the last image: ring the bell and step into the portal.

Hallowgrove is the map most new players will see first. Xbox Wire calls it the bleached Hallowgrove and pairs it with the fortress of Brandrgarde. Soul Harvest can trigger on both maps, but you do not need the ritual for a first extract. Brandrgarde-Cataclysm is a punishing extra difficulty from the launch release, not a tutorial, and Brandrgarde Solo Mode is not live until mid Season 1 in September.

How to spend your first hour

Finish whatever onboarding the client gives you. Steam’s July 30 notes mention a Sorcerer tutorial inside the beginner guide, which confirms that an in-game guide exists. Read it. This fan page is not a replacement for that UI.

Queue a normal-intensity map and travel light. Official systems that matter immediately are your loadout, warehouse, and Pip’s Pouches and Keychains. Steam has patched players who tried to change Pip’s Pouches after matchmaking started, so lock your bag before you queue. Official news never published a “safe” gear budget, so if you cannot replace an item, do not take it into the mist.

Fight Corroded packs only until you understand stagger and dodge. The website says treasures are guarded by the Corroded or by Mist Lords, and that hunting other Gyldhunters is the quicker, greedier route. For a first extract, other hunters are a threat, not a farm. Illegal Teaming is a real ban category, but it means pre-made squads who entered the same match to ruin others.

When you have any haul you would hate to lose, start the Woodling. Confirm the Soul of Return. Summon at the Soul Tree without wandering in and out of the area, because launch notes say that path could spend an extra soul. Leave, then move loot into the warehouse before you queue again.

Season 1 systems you will see immediately

Season 1 is Soul Hunt. The launch release promised a free seven-day login event that unlocks the Slumbering Contract at no cost. Soul Harvest, the season ritual, is already live as of 12 August 2026, 08:00 UTC. Fate Chart is the seasonal task board, and Path of Glory appears in settlement-screen notes.

Einherjar mode lets you challenge Mist Lords with Dried Flower Knots, which started dropping after the August 6 update. You do not need any of those systems for extract number one. They are the live-service layer on top of the loop. If the Fate Chart or a Soul Cocoon appears during your first match, you can ignore it and still leave through a Woodling.

Fair play is not optional flavor. Bellring publishes regular penalty lists for cheating and Illegal Teaming. Report suspects in-game. There are no official redeem codes.

Common beginner mistakes

Do not treat community damage tables as official. Bellring’s own patches speak in “slightly increased” and “slightly reduced,” and they say classes missing from a patch are still under review. There is no official beginner DPS ranking.

Do not wait for Brandrgarde Solo Mode. It is dated September, mid Season 1, for Normal and Cataclysm. It is not a current queue. Do not invent a camp name, a smuggler exit, or a ferry if the official tutorial in front of you does not show them.

Do not skip the extract because a seasonal altar looks more important. The store’s win condition is still a full haul returned to camp. Soul Harvest rewards are real, but they sit on top of a match you can still fail.
